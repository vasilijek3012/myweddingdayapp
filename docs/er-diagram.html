Reflects the decisions from this session: visitors stay account-less (only owner and band roles authenticate); a dedicated Payments table replaces the bare is_active flag for a real transaction history; listings support both a flat one-time fee and a time-limited deal via a nullable active_until; photos move from a single image_url column into a proper Images table; and Reviews are moderated — nothing shows publicly until is_approved is set.

No user_id — reviewers have no account, so name/email are captured free-text on the row. Defaults to unapproved; a review only appears publicly once a moderator (owner/admin) flips is_approved, which stamps approved_at.

Open questions this doesn't resolve

Who moderates reviews?
Reviews need an approver. Today there's no admin role in the Users CHECK — either add one, or let the listing's own owner/band approve reviews of their own listing (weaker, since it lets vendors hide criticism).
Stripe checkout/webhook routes
Still explicitly deferred per CLAUDE.md — Payments' shape above is ready for it, but wiring stays out of scope until asked.
Expiry job for time-limited deals
If active_until is used, something needs to flip is_active=false once it passes — a cron/scheduled task, not a DB trigger, to stay consistent with "no stored procedures."
